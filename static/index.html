SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Built for Academic Excellence

A comprehensive platform that bridges administration and students through intelligent scheduling and meaningful feedback collection.

📅

Dynamic Timetable Builder

Create, edit, and manage semester timetables with drag-and-drop simplicity. Assign subjects, rooms, and time slots with conflict detection.

📄

Instant PDF Export

Generate beautifully formatted timetable PDFs with college branding. Download instantly or share digitally with one click.

📝

Smart Feedback System

Build dynamic feedback forms with rating, text, and MCQ question types. Share selectively to individual students and track completion.

🔐

Role-Based Security

JWT-secured authentication with Admin and Student roles. Every endpoint protected with role verification and access control.

📊

Response Analytics

View aggregated feedback responses per form or drill down to individual student submissions with clean tabular display.

🏫

Room Management

Maintain a complete inventory of classrooms and labs with capacity tracking to optimize space allocation across departments.

How It Works

ADMIN
01

Register & Login

Create an admin account and log in with your credentials to access the dashboard.

02

Setup Subjects & Rooms

Add all subjects with coordinator names and register available rooms with capacities.

03

Build Timetable

Assign subjects to time slots, days, rooms, and sections to build the complete schedule.

04

Create Feedback Forms

Design dynamic forms with custom questions — ratings, text responses, or MCQ options.

05

Share & Analyze

Share forms to individual students and review aggregated or per-student responses.

STUDENT
01

Register & Login

Create a student account and log in to access your personalized dashboard.

02

View Timetable

Access your complete class schedule for the semester, neatly organized by day and time.

03

Download PDF

Export your timetable as a professionally formatted PDF for offline reference.

04

Fill Feedback Forms

Complete feedback forms assigned by admin with dynamic question interfaces.

05

Track Submissions

View pending and completed feedback assignments from your dashboard.
